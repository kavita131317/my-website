About Jaipur

Jaipur is the capital city of Rajasthan and is popularly known as the Pink City.

Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, Jaipur is famous for its magnificent forts, royal palaces, colorful markets and rich cultural heritage.

The city is an important part of India's Golden Triangle, along with Delhi and Agra.

Experience Jaipur Culture

Jaipur is a wonderful combination of royal traditions, colorful festivals, folk music, traditional handicrafts, delicious food and beautiful Rajasthani art. From vibrant bazaars to magnificent palaces, every corner of Jaipur tells a story of Rajasthan's rich heritage.
